Data notes

  • Corporate financials (Balance Sheet, Financial Statement, Budget, Labor/Marketing/Overhead Expenses) are stated in thousands of US dollars and tie together internally — e.g. 2018 total labor expense ($364.1M) matches the Salaries and Wages line in the Financial Statement exactly.
  • The e-commerce transaction files and the Product Line catalog are a separate dataset, in actual dollars, and do not reconcile to the corporate financial statements — treat them as an operational/CRM snapshot, not GAAP-consistent revenue.
  • The Balance Sheet's "Balance check" row never resolves to exactly zero (off by ~$0.30–0.44K each year) — rounding artifacts in the source file, not a real imbalance.
  • Overhead Costs.xlsx's 2018 tab title reads "Red30 Tech 2017 Overhead Costs" — a copy-paste label error in the source; the monthly dates and figures on that tab are for 2018.
  • marketing_rubric.pdf in this folder is unrelated to the Red30 Tech dataset series — a generic document-scoring rubric, likely a stray file.
